Delete Your Auction Tracker Account

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and data for Auction Tracker, the mobile app developed by Amara Tsumugi.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to amaratsumugi@gmail.com with the subject "Auction Tracker account deletion".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Auction Tracker, so we can confirm the account belongs to you.
  3. We will delete your account and reply to confirm once it is done.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address and password)
  • All auctions you created
  • All cars and their details (lot numbers, car names, prices, statuses and any custom fields)
  • Your custom columns and custom car names

All of this is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days of your request. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Data stored locally on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

See our Privacy Policy for more on how your data is handled.
