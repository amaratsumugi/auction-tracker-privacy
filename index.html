Privacy Policy for Auction Tracker

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Auction Tracker ("the App") is developed by Amara Tsumugi ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide for authentication. Password are never stored in readable form; authentication is handled by our backend provider, Supabase.

The App stores the auction, car, and custom field data you enter (for example: auction names, dates, car details, prices, and notes) so it can sync across your devices. This data is linked to your account.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or device identifiers beyond what is technically required to operate the App (such as a session token stored on your device).

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account
  • To sync your auction and car data across the devices you sign in on
  • To operate core features such as printing and search

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage & Third Parties

Your account and data are stored using Supabase (Postgres database, authentication, and realtime sync infrastructure). Supabase acts as our data processor. You can review Supabase's own privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

On your device, a local copy of your data is kept in an offline-first local database (SQLite) so the App works without an internet connection.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, follow the steps on our account deletion page or contact us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
